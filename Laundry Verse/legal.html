Terms & Policies

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Damage Protection Policy

We exercise the utmost care in processing articles entrusted to us. However, we are not responsible for:

  • Color bleeding from non-fast fabrics.
  • Shrinkage of fabrics that are not pre-shrunk.
  • Damage to buttons, beads, or zippers (though we try to protect them).

Guarantee: In case of loss or damage directly caused by our operational error, our liability is limited to 10 times the processing charge of that individual item.

2. Delivery Policy

Standard delivery time is 24-48 hours. Express delivery (same day) is available at a 50% surcharge. We are not liable for delays caused by unforeseen circumstances (weather, strikes, etc.).

3. Re-Wash Policy

If you are unsatisfied with the cleaning quality, you must notify us within 24 hours of delivery and keep the item unused with tags intact. We will re-process the item free of charge.
