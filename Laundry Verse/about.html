Redefining Laundry in Indore

Laundryverse started with a simple mission: to make premium hygiene accessible. We aren't just a dhobi ghat; we are a tech-enabled fabric care center located in the heart of Vijay Nagar.

Our facility uses state-of-the-art machines that consume less water and eco-friendly detergents that keep your clothes softer for longer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my clothes get damaged?

We have a strict 100% damage protection policy. We offer compensation up to 10 times the processing charge.

What chemicals do you use?

We use eco-friendly, hypoallergenic bio-liquids from certified German brands. Tough on stains, gentle on skin.

How fast is delivery?

Standard delivery is 48 hours. We also offer 'Express 24H' service for urgent requirements.

Do you mix my clothes with others?

Never. Each customer's laundry is processed in a separate individual machine load.
